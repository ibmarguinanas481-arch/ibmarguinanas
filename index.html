Hello! I'm Ibmar M. Guinanas, a recent Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS) graduate based in Siocon ZDN, Philippines. I have a strong passion for building web-based systems that solve real, practical problems for real people and organizations.

My development work leans heavily on the PHP + MySQL + AJAX stack, which lets me build fast, offline-capable systems ideal for local government offices, small businesses, and institutions that need reliable solutions without depending on a constant internet connection.

My most recent project is an IoT-based Soil Plant Monitoring System using ESP32 sensors paired with a web dashboard — a project that pushed me to go beyond traditional web development and explore hardware-software integration.
